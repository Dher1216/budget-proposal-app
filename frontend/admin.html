Special Purpose Appropriations (mandatory deductions)

These are deducted from Available Budget before offices are allocated anything — the 20% Development Fund and 5% LDRRMF are computed automatically from the fund sources above; Aid to Barangays defaults to ₱590,000 but can be changed; you can add other one-off deductions too.

Prorate an account by number of months

For accounts like "Other General Services" or "Other Professional Services" used for Job Order / Contract of Service salaries — reduce the whole-year proposed amount down to fund only a set number of months, across every office at once. This writes into each office's Adjusted Proposal (and its remarks), leaving the original Proposed amount untouched.
